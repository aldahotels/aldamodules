AI tools specific to Alda Hotels, served over MCP with the guardrails of
``pms_ai_tool``:

* ``alda_payroll_465_preview`` and ``alda_payroll_465_reconcile``: matching and
  reconciliation of the payroll accounts 465 by the 7-digit employee number.
* ``alda_bank_pending_lines``, ``alda_bank_match_candidates``,
  ``alda_bank_notify_taz`` and ``alda_bank_reconcile``: reconciliation of the
  CaixaBank statements of each hotel against the grouped TPV entries (``TB``)
  and the transfer collections (``CB``), the notice to the hotel's TAZ and the
  write-off of cents differences to 678/778.

The write tools need a preview and an explicit confirmation, a list of allowed
users and the switches of ``pms_ai_tool`` turned on.

Configuration
=============

System parameters, all optional:

* ``alda_ai_tool.bank_journal_prefix`` (``CB``) and
  ``alda_ai_tool.tpv_journal_prefix`` (``TB``): codes of the hotel's bank and
  TPV journals; the digits after the prefix are the hotel code.
* ``alda_ai_tool.cents_threshold`` (``0.05``): largest difference written off.
* ``alda_ai_tool.writeoff_expense_account_code`` (``67800000000``) and
  ``alda_ai_tool.writeoff_income_account_code`` (``77800000000``).
* ``alda_ai_tool.taz_job_ids``: jobs that make an employee the TAZ of a hotel.
  By default, the jobs whose name contains "TAZ".

The hotel of an employee comes from the employee's properties
(``pms_hr_property``); the analytic account of the write-off, from the
property.
